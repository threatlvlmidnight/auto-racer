# Contract: Demo Feedback Bug Pass

## 1. Race feedback boundary

- Simulation emits immutable stat/contribution changes at deterministic lap or
  activation boundaries.
- Playback publishes each crossed boundary once in canonical order.
- Presentation may animate highlight/arrow/source text but cannot recompute or
  mutate values, pace, position, or settlement.

## 2. Item inspection boundary

- One catalog-backed formatter exposes complete rule text, all tags, scaling
  classification, current inputs, synergy targets/thresholds, and resolved
  values.
- Tag preview/pin returns matching held locations for board/storage highlights.
- All meaning is available through focus/selection; hover is optional enhancement.

## 3. Acquisition boundary

- Successful purchase consumes its stock entry exactly once and leaves a
  disabled receipt in that position.
- Restock atomically replaces all three positions with fresh eligible offer IDs.
- Duplicate tier commit returns old/new tier and changed effect values; feedback
  never performs a second tier mutation.
- Reward `SKIP REWARDS` delegates to the existing no-acquisition transition.

## 4. Inventory and sale boundary

- Inventory can open from every active-run non-playback host only when no other
  modal/transaction is unresolved.
- A typed host token restores selection, focus, navigation, offers, and setup
  state except mutations explicitly committed in inventory.
- Measured safe bounds choose overlay/full-window; actions are identical.
- Drag reveals sell affordance. Drop commits one authoritative sale and creates
  one bounded Undo snapshot. Undo is invalid after another mutation/transition.

## 5. Settlement and economy boundary

- Third place yields +1 reputation for Local, Championship, and Elite scored
  races without changing purse or championship points.
- Held economy items scan installed and stored locations at transaction time:
  Chit `+tier` on positions 1–3, Nameplate `+tier` per sale, Plaque `+2×tier` on
  successful sponsor completion.
- Each receipt names source, tier, location, and amount. No live randomness.

## 6. Record boundary

- Final wins/losses derive from retained scored history, not scene counters.
- Local, Championship, and Elite Finale count once; 1–3 win, 4–8 loss.
- Points and reputation remain independently displayed.

## 7. Balance boundary

- Fixed fixtures and seeds are versioned and reproducible.
- Nell content and shared baseline vehicle stats are immutable controls.
- Existing Evelyn/Lucien/Inez exclusive values/synergies may increase or be
  recomposed. New items require documented failing evidence from both gates.
- Acceptance requires ≤5 percentage-point representative rate spread and ≤2%
  optimized ceiling spread.

## 8. UI chrome boundary

- Named crop/nine-slice regions come from the approved transparent master.
- Runtime labels, icons, focus, enabled state, and actions remain semantic code.
- Color is never the sole state signal. Supported viewports must not clip or
  overlap. Asset styling remains subordinate to items/build/race action.

## 9. Explicit exclusions

- No cross-race/day scaling item, crash, driver signature/skill, overtake rule,
  comeback rule, multiplayer service, global mobile redesign, or live contest
  input is introduced.
