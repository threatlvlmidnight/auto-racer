## Constitutional finding

Constitution Principle V says players must have low-stakes build-testing access.
With every player-facing Test Day entry hidden and no replacement provided,
Feature 045 temporarily fails that player-access requirement even though all
Test Day code/tests remain. The plan records this honestly as an owner-directed,
reversible deviation and centralizes one visibility flag. The handoff must not
claim Principle V is satisfied. Before a constitution-compliant release, the
product must restore Test Day, provide another qualifying access path, or amend
the constitution through governance.

This is not a coding ambiguity and does not prevent the requested implementation
package from being precise; it is an explicit product-governance consequence.
