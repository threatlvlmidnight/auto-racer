# Feature 042 Clarification Questionnaire: Item Pool Remediation, Expansion, and Loot

**Re-created**: 2026-08-17 after the three-perspective current-item review  
**Status**: Complete — owner selected Q1A–Q7A, Q8C, Q9A, and Q10A on
2026-08-17, with recorded Q1/Q5/Q7 amendments

Reply with one choice per question, for example: `Q1 A, Q2 A, Q3 A, Q4 A,
Q5 A, Q6 A, Q7 A, Q8 A, Q9 A, Q10 A`. Amend any answer in free form.

The recommendations deliberately favor improving effective variety rather than
maximizing raw item count.

## Q1 — How much of the existing pool should Feature 042 retrofit?

**A — Repair defects plus retrofit 8–12 items (recommended).** Fix the dead
cards and misleading presentation, then revise roughly two or three current
items per origin into clearer payoffs, bridges, tradeoffs, sequencing effects,
or meaningful Fitted/Improvised decisions. This gets more value from art already
planned for the current roster.

**B — Repair defects plus retrofit 4–6 items.** Touch only the clearest
mechanical gaps. This reduces balance churn but leaves much of the current pool
as direct-stat variations.

**C — Correctness repairs only.** Fix dead/misreported items but preserve all
other current mechanics. New content must carry the entire expansion.

Regardless of choice, Trade Ledger Chit and Bookmaker's Declared Margin cannot
remain dead offers, and tag/economy/target/cooldown copy must become truthful.

## Q2 — How many new items should follow those retrofits?

**A — 8 active + 4 Loot (recommended with Q1A).** Two active payoff/bridge
items per origin plus four Loot definitions. This creates a real expansion while
keeping reachable pools near 28 items under an origin-specific distribution.

**B — 4 active + 4 Loot.** One active item per origin plus four Loot. Best if
Q1A's retrofits do most of the mechanical work and cognitive load is the main
constraint.

**C — 12 active + 4 Loot.** Three active items per origin plus four Loot. Best
only if existing-item retrofits are intentionally limited.

## Q3 — How should the 19 current tags be treated?

**A — Preserve IDs, classify a smaller payoff-bearing family set
(recommended).** Keep stable tags for compatibility, but explicitly label which
are build families, cross-origin bridge/utility tags, and flavor/acquisition
descriptors. Only build-family tags promise dedicated payoff density.

**B — Consolidate to roughly 12 mechanical tags.** Merge or retire overlapping
and one-carrier tags. This gives the cleanest long-term vocabulary but requires
more catalog, copy, Tag Specialist, sponsor, and compatibility migration.

**C — Make all 19 tags real build families.** Add payoffs for every current tag.
This preserves vocabulary but greatly expands content and balance obligations.

Under A, the proposed payoff lines are craftsmanship/restoration, cadence/
sprint, controlled-limit engineering, loophole/exposure/evasion, and selected
cross-origin bridges such as Information + conditional equipment.

## Q4 — What should happen to exact-count Synergies?

**A — Make exact-count primarily a Voss signature (recommended).** Preserve a
small number of highly visible rulebook/loophole constraints for Voss. Convert
Soto and Rook's current exact-two effects to at-least, range, or graduated
thresholds so acquiring another matching item does not simply switch them off.

**B — Keep all current exact counts.** Improve live preview so the player sees
that another match disables the effect. This preserves the pruning puzzle but
retains its trap potential.

**C — Remove exact-count shutoff entirely.** Convert every current effect to
at-least/range/graduated behavior. This is friendliest but loses a thematic Voss
mechanic.

## Q5 — How should Buff duplicates scale with tier?

**A — Multiplicative 15%/30%, matching direct stats (recommended).** A 10% Buff
becomes 11.5%/13%, and a 3% stacking Buff becomes 3.45%/3.9%. This removes the
current disproportionate jump while preserving universal tier language.

**B — Keep +15/+30 percentage points.** A 3% Buff becomes 18%/33%. This makes
Buff duplicates explosive chase outcomes and requires balancing every Buff
around that behavior.

**C — Author tier increments per item.** Each Buff defines its tier-2/tier-3
values. This offers maximum control but adds substantial content/UI complexity.

## Q6 — What should rarity mean, and how should normal offers behave?

**A — Rarity drives weighted availability and consistent design bands
(recommended).** Standard appears most often, Notable less often, and Rare least
often; rarity also communicates increasing complexity/build-around commitment,
not guaranteed raw power. Every three-card offer still suppresses duplicate IDs.

**B — Rename rarity as a non-probability quality/complexity label.** Keep
uniform draws but stop using conventional rarity language that implies odds.

**C — Keep display-only rarity with explicit disclosure.** Preserve current
uniform behavior and teach that rarity is presentation only.

Duplicate definitions within one visible offer will be suppressed regardless
of this answer.

## Q7 — How should players acquire the four Loot types?

**A — Shared weighted/capped Loot lane (selected).** All entrants can access all
four stat Loot definitions through explicitly neutral acquisition events,
neutral-only shops, or authored rewards that opt into Loot. Loot never appears
in a normal entrant-origin shop/draft, and an eligible neutral offer contains at
most one Loot slot rather than four unrestricted uniform cards.

**B — One exclusive Loot per origin in normal pools.** Each entrant normally
sees one stat Loot and can obtain others through Cross-Pollination/Tag
Specialist. This strengthens origin identity and raises each entrant pool by one.

**C — Four shared Neutral Loot items in normal pools.** Every entrant can see
all four through ordinary draws, with a one-Loot-per-offer cap. This is simple
but still dilutes active choices more often than A.

## Q8 — Where is Loot held, and what is the conversion called?

**A — Normal capacity; `Apply`/`Trade In`; permanent bonus only
(recommended).** Loot can occupy installed or storage positions but is always
inert and never Fitted/Improvised/adjacent. Converting it grants no normal sale
credits, so the UI avoids calling it a sale.

**B — Storage only; `Apply`; permanent bonus only.** Loot consumes workshop
storage but cannot displace an active vehicle item. This is easier to explain
but weakens capacity/rearrangement pressure.

**C — Normal capacity; `Sell`; half-price credits plus bonus.** Preserve normal
sale language and payout. This is intuitive economically but risks making Loot
strictly high-value.

## Q9 — What happens when duplicate Loot is acquired?

**A — Normal tiering; tier 1/2/3 applies +1/+2/+3 points (recommended).** This
uses the existing duplicate language, preserves bounded inventory, and ensures
a merged duplicate gains conversion value.

**B — Separate consumable instances.** Identical Loot never merges; each stays
at +1 and occupies capacity. This is mechanically clean for consumables but is
an exception to normal duplicate rules.

**C — Reject or convert duplicates.** A held matching Loot prevents another
copy or converts it directly to credits. This avoids tiered consumables but
creates special acquisition behavior.

## Q10 — Confirm target, stacking, Undo, and lifecycle defaults

**A — Accept the reviewed defaults (recommended).** Traverse authored vehicle
slots then storage indexes; choose the first non-Loot item with an authored or
Workshop-modification contribution to that stat; earlier Loot bonuses alone do
not create eligibility. Bonuses add up to +3 per target instance/stat. Immediate
Undo reverses the entire transaction atomically. The bonus follows retained
identity through moves, storage, tiering, and modifications, and leaves the run
when that identity is sold/surrendered/replaced. Transformations preserve it
only when they explicitly preserve the same instance.

**B — Player chooses among eligible targets.** Keep the same applicability,
cap, Undo, and lifecycle rules, but replace automatic leftmost targeting with a
manual recipient choice. This is more flexible but removes the topology puzzle.

**C — Submit an amended rule.** Specify a different target order,
applicability, cap, Undo rule, or identity lifecycle.

## Locked regardless of answers

- The two unintended dead cards and misleading card facts are repaired or
  explicitly removed before expansion approval.
- Visible multi-card offers contain distinct definition IDs.
- The final roster passes catalog, offer-distribution, duplicate/tier-attainment,
  and effective-mechanic audits.
- Loot is typed and completely inert before conversion.
- Loot cannot participate in adjacency, Buffs, Synergies, setup, sponsors, Tag
  Specialist, Workshop Modifications, Scrutineering, Rebuild, or contest events.
- Loot conversion is deterministic, previewable, versioned, idempotent, and
  all-or-nothing.
- Permanent bonuses are normalized run-scoped instance ledger entries, never
  mutations of shared item definitions and never multiplicatively amplified.
- Feature 037 production art waits for the approved Feature 042 roster.
- DeepSeek receives no image generation, asset approval, screenshot capture, or
  qualitative manual-verification work.
